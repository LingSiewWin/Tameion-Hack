Your bills, paid on time, inside a limit you set.

Tami reads what your company already pays, pays it when due, and can prove every payment. Your phone is the only key that can take money out.

The agent's key can pay payees you approved, under a cap, and nothing else. It cannot add a payee, raise its own limit, or withdraw. You sign; Tami holds no key that can take your money.
