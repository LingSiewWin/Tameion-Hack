Level 1: your own policy, your phone signs

A contract holds the money. Your passkey is the only key that can add a payee or withdraw. The agent reads what you owe and prepares each payment; nothing moves until you tap. Raising any limit waits the delay and you can cancel it.

When you are ready for Level 2

    
Autopilot pays approved payees inside the caps without a tap. Your passkey asks for it; the contract schedules the raise behind the delay, shows it here, and your passkey can cancel it until then. Lowering (Propose, or Watch to pause) applies the moment you sign. The service's own key can pause you, and can never raise you without the same delay.
