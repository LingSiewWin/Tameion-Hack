Arbitrum Sepolia → Circle Gateway → Arc testnet policy, 2026-10-14

What: the account page lists the chains a browser wallet can deposit into Circle Gateway from
(`GET /api/level1/config` → gatewaySources: Ethereum Sepolia, OP Sepolia, Arbitrum Sepolia, Base
Sepolia, Arc Testnet — domain, EVM chain id, USDC contract, the Gateway wallet contract
0x0077777d7EBA4688BDeF3E311b846F25870A19B9, one address on every chain). The owner picks a chain and
an amount. The wallet switches to that chain and sends approve(gateway, amount) + deposit(usdc,
amount) as ONE wallet_sendCalls batch (version 2.0.0, atomicRequired:false; the page polls
wallet_getCallsStatus until status 200), or as two eth_sendTransaction calls when the wallet has no
EIP-5792. The deposit shows on the Elsewhere card as "arriving" until Circle credits it; then
"Bring it to the policy" (PLAN 5.15) mints it into the Arc policy. Tameion never touches the money:
it goes from the wallet into Circle's Gateway contract under the owner's own address; the page only
encodes two calls and names Circle's contract.
