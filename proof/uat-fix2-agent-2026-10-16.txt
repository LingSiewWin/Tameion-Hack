WHAT /agent IS NOW (apps/console/src/app/agent/* only)
  Always, from the account's own record: Mandate (left now, spent in 24 h against the daily cap as a meter, per payment, per day, what the account holds,
  the mode in words, a waiting change in words) · Pass & anchor (halt, last pass and what it read, pass latency, last anchor read from the account's
  anchored records with its transaction, agent key lifetime) · Payments (time, payee by the account's own name, the reason the agent gave, USDC, receipt) ·
  Refused (the checks' reason in words, and why the agent wanted it) · Asked of you (what the agents' keys asked, with their reasons) · Events.
  Only when the desk's feed names a connected exchange account: Positions, Orders & read-back, Margin, Funding, Hedge (halt, passes).
  Data sources: GET /api/level1/inbox (caps.spent24h and caps.leftNow, paid, purchases, refused, waiting, agentAsks, lastPass), GET /api/level1/records
  (the last anchored record: time, event count, anchorTx), GET /api/watch/<lastPass.id> (the pass's own progress.elapsedSecs), the event stream (and it
  refetches the inbox and the records the moment an event arrives, so a purchase shows with its amount, seller and reason). Nothing from the desk's
  feed is used for the anchor, the pass or the payments.
