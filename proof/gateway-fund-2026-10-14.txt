Fund the policy from Circle Gateway (PLAN 5.15, docs/56 #3) — proved on Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: the bill wallet's USDC held in Circle Gateway (on any chain Gateway
serves) moves into the policy in one signed intent. The service prepares the
EIP-712 burn intent with Circle's fee and expiry (POST /v1/estimate); the
bill wallet signs it (on the page: eth_signTypedData_v4 in the browser
wallet; here: `cast wallet sign --data --from-file` with the treasury key
file); the service hands it to Gateway (POST /v1/transfer) and relays
GatewayMinter.gatewayMint(attestation, signature) on Arc with the relayer
key, which only pays gas. The service refuses an intent whose
destinationRecipient is not that tenant's policy on its chain.

Sources read first (2026-10-14): developers.circle.com/gateway/quickstarts/unified-balance-evm
(domain {name:"GatewayWallet",version:"1"} with no chainId; BurnIntent /
TransferSpec types and field order; bytes32 left-padded addresses;
destinationCaller zero = anyone may mint), /openapi/gateway.yaml (field
types, /v1/transfer answers {attestation, signature, transferId, fees,
expirationBlock}), /gateway/references/contract-addresses (one wallet
0x0077…19B9 and one minter 0x0022…475B on every EVM testnet; Arc = 26).

Learned by running it, not in the docs we read:
- /v1/estimate on testnet answers a bare array [{burnIntent:{maxBlockHeight,
  maxFee, spec}}], not the OpenAPI's {body, fees}; the parser takes both.
- Cloudflare in front of the API refused a client with no User-Agent
  ("error sending request"); with a User-Agent it answers.
- The fee is charged on top of the value: "Insufficient balance for
  depositor …: available 0.050000, required 0.0535" (maxFee 3850 minor;
  actual fee 0.0035). "All of it" therefore = balance - maxFee (`all:true`).
