Where the agent reports, set by the owner's passkey (PLAN 5.3, the "treasurer-set
address" half) — proved on the hosted service against Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: until today only the host's admin token could point a tenant's
after-pass reports (signed webhook, Telegram) somewhere. Now the owner does
it from `/` with the same passkey that moves money: the service issues a
32-byte challenge = keccak256("tameion.notify.v1" || chainId || policy ||
url || telegramChatId || issued); the browser signs it with
navigator.credentials.get; the service verifies the WebAuthn assertion
itself — `webauthn.get`, the exact challenge in clientDataJSON, P-256 over
sha256(authenticatorData || sha256(clientDataJSON)) — against the key the
tenant's PasskeyPrincipal holds on chain (`qx()`, `qy()` read live), refuses
stale (>10 min) or reused challenges, then stores the address and mints the
webhook secret, shown once to the setter. A different receiver URL gets a
different secret. Nothing on chain; no admin.

Code: `level1::notify_challenge`, `level1::verify_passkey_assertion` (p256
0.13, sha2), `level1::apply_notify`, `arc::ArcClient::passkey_key`,
`POST /api/level1/prepare-notify`, `POST /api/level1/notify-signed`; card
"Where the agent reports" on `/`. Unit tests: a P-256 assertion over the
right challenge verifies, a different challenge or one flipped bit in
authenticatorData does not, an off-curve key is refused; base64url vectors;
secret minted on first URL, kept for the same URL, re-minted for a new one.
