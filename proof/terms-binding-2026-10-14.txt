Approvals bound to the terms the owner saw, and to a deadline (PLAN 5.23, docs/59 §3.11:
the ERC-5646 state-fingerprint and EIP-5081 expiring-transaction ideas) — Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: an approval is a decision about a state — these caps, this autonomy level,
this agent key, this operator, this delay, this agent expiry, this pending change.
The policy exposes `fingerprint()` = keccak256(abi.encode(perActionCap, dailyCap,
reserveFloor, uint8 mode, agent, operator, delay, agentExpiry, pending.eta,
pending.kinds)). `settleAsPrincipalIf(to, amount, actionHash, terms, deadline)` and
`setPayeesIf(payees, attested, terms, deadline)` do what the plain calls do, but
revert `TermsChanged(signed, current)` if the fingerprint moved between reading
and landing, and `ApprovalExpired(deadline)` after the deadline. The service
binds every Needs-you row to the live fingerprint for ten minutes; the page
recomputes the fingerprint from the fields the service hands it (`terms`) and
refuses to sign a call bound to anything else; the contract refuses again if the
terms move before the transaction lands. The plain calls stay for older policies.

Tests: contracts/test/BudgetPolicyTerms.t.sol, 6 — the fingerprint moves with every
term (pending raise, mode, caps, agent expiry) and not with a payment; a bound
settlement pays while the terms hold; is refused by name once the operator raised
the limits in between; is refused after its deadline; bound payee approval holds
the same line; only the principal, and the plain settle still works. forge: 124.
arc: `Terms::recompute` pinned to `cast abi-encode … | cast keccak` =
0x95a4f8627252855cd26640bbeec95e9a4d924e1c6bceef775d858778547ad43c; the page's
`termsFingerprint` pinned to the same vector; decoder words for both bound calls.
