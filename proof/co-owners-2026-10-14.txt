Several devices, several people: co-owner passkeys and M-of-N approvals (PLAN 5.7) — Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: the owner contract now holds more than one passkey — a second phone, a co-founder, the CFO —
and a threshold of how many must approve every change. Key 0 is the first passkey (the one recovery
and guardians rotate); keys 1..n are co-owners. At threshold one any key approves alone; at two or
more, one key is refused by the contract (`NeedsMoreApprovals`) and the service collects approvals
from distinct keys over the same challenge, then relays them together (`executeWith`). Only the
account changes its owners, through a call its current owners approved; recovery returns a stranded
2-of-2 to one key (whoever can recover could already replace the key). A new device asks to join
from the account link; the owners add it from their page. The page decodes "add an owner" in words
before anyone signs: "Owners: your passkey and 1 more device; 2 of 2 must approve every change".

Code: contracts/src/PasskeyPrincipal.sol (coQx/coQy, approvalThreshold, setCoOwners, keyAt,
executeAs, executeWith, `_run`; applyRecovery resets); contracts/test/PasskeyCoOwners.t.sol (5:
second device alone at threshold 1; 2-of-2 refuses one key and runs with both; the same key twice,
the wrong key and unordered indexes refused; only the account changes its owners and the set is
checked; recovery returns a 2-of-2 to one key); forge 157. arc: owner_signers, execute_as_key,
execute_with_keys, set_co_owners_calldata. level1: execute_by_key (assertion verified off-chain
against the claimed key before anything is stored), PendingApproval in tenants/<owner>/approvals.json
(stale when the nonce moves). Service: execute takes keyIndex and answers 202 {pending, have, need}
until the threshold is met; POST /api/level1/join-request (open: the device holds no key yet);
POST /api/level1/prepare-coowners; inbox owners {threshold, keys}, approvals, joinRequests. Page:
"Who owns this account" card (ask to join, add as owner with a threshold choice, approve what waits),
sign() sends this device's key index. Decoder + test; ERC-7730 entries (setCoOwners, executeAs,
executeWith). New owner contracts get this code; owners deployed before keep theirs (one key).

Chain of custody: two devices, two keys in the owner contract, a threshold of two. One person's
approval moved nothing; the second person's approval of the same call, at the same nonce, ran it
once. The service only carried the two signatures; it could neither forge one nor run the call with one.
