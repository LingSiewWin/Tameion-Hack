Standing orders (PLAN 5.25, docs/59 §3.3: the EIP-7833 idea) — Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: a bill the owner confirmed is written into the policy contract as a
standing order — payee, amount, cadence, next due date. When it falls due,
ANYONE may call payDue(id): the agent on its pass, the treasurer from the
page, a cron job, a stranger. The contract checks the payee is still approved
and cooled off, the per-action and daily caps, the balance and the floor;
pays one cycle; advances the order by one interval; emits the same Paid event
with scheduleActionHash(id, dueAt) as the replay key. Refused while the policy
is paused (Watch). Ending an order is instant for the principal or the
operator. Tameion's server being down no longer means a bill goes unpaid.

Contract: contracts/src/BudgetPolicy.sol — Schedule struct and schedules[],
addSchedule (principal; payee attested, amount ≤ per-action cap, cadence ≥ 1 h),
removeSchedule (principal or operator), payDue (anyone), scheduleActionHash;
the agent's own checks factored into _payChecked and shared. Tests:
contracts/test/BudgetPolicySchedule.t.sol, 9 — only the principal adds and
the payee must be attested; anyone pays a due order in Propose and the order
advances; not-due is refused by name; missed cycles pay one call at a time;
paused refuses and a removed order never pays; the daily cap still bounds six
orders of 100 at 500 (and a refused call does not advance the order); a
revoked payee stops its order; the agent cannot replay a cycle an order paid;
the agent's own pay still needs Autopilot. forge: 112 tests.

Operator: on every pass, due standing orders are paid first (Propose too — the
owner already said yes to exactly this), read back like any payment (HALT on
mismatch), recorded as Settlement with cycle_key schedule:<id>:<dueAt>; bills an
order covers are skipped, not proposed again. Service: inbox standingOrders and
standingOrderCandidates (confirmed or imported bills, cadence ≥ 1 h, no order
yet); routes prepare-schedule (passkey calldata; refused in words when the
payee is not approved), prepare-unschedule, pay-due (relayer's gas). Page:
"Standing orders" card — the agent's candidates with "Make it standing" (one
passkey tap), each order with "Pay now" when due and "End it". scripts/pay_due.sh
<policy> <id|list>. MCP: prepare_schedule. The page's clear-signing decoder
describes addSchedule and removeSchedule in words (and refuses to describe
payDue: that is anyone's call, never the passkey's).
