One passkey, many accounts, any device (PLAN 5.35) — 2026-10-14

What: lazy deploy (5.32) makes a second policy under the same passkey cheap, so the account page
now (1) lists every account this browser knows — "Your accounts: 0x7839…0df9 (holds money) ·
0x2ecd…e8a3 (from your wallet) · …" — and switches with one tap (`tameion.tenants` in localStorage,
`accountsLine()`); (2) opens an existing account from a link that carries only the policy address
(`/?policy=0x…` → `GET /api/level1/tenant/<policy>` → remembered → shown), for a phone or a fresh
browser — the passkey, synced by the platform, is what signs there; (3) "Open on another device"
in the footer copies that link.

Found on the way (docs/60 P-17, security): `GET /api/level1/tenant/<policy>` served the whole tenant
file, including `notify_secret` (the HMAC key of the owner's webhook) and the agent-key hashes.
Fixed before shipping the link: `Tenant::public()` strips both (adds `notifySet`,
`agentTokenCount`); the deploy reply and the admin list use it too; unit test
`a_public_tenant_carries_no_secret`.
  curl /api/level1/tenant/0x1645951793f7ca2ae36d7a3eeae90ef93e1932ab (a tenant with a webhook):
    before: notify_secret present (True)   after: notify_secret in body False, agent_tokens False,
    notifySet True.
