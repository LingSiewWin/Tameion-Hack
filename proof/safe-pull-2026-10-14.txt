Connect your Safe (PLAN 5.27, docs/59 §3.1: the Zodiac idea, taken the cheap way) — Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: onchain companies keep their money in a Safe. With pull mode (PLAN 5.26)
nothing else is needed: the Safe is the bill wallet, one Safe transaction
approves the policy an allowance, and the agent pulls each payment from the
Safe inside the caps, payee list, modes and delays — the Safe's signers can
cut it to zero with another approve(policy, 0) at any time, without Tameion.
No module, no new trust path into the Safe: the policy can take exactly what
the allowance says and nothing else. (A Zodiac-style module that calls the
Safe directly is the fuller form; this is the form that works today, on Arc,
with the Safe contracts Safe itself deployed there.)

Facts first (proof/chain-facts-arc-2026-10-14.txt §6): Safe 1.4.1 and 1.3.0
singletons and proxy factories are deployed on Arc testnet at their canonical
addresses — docs/59 had this unverified.

allowance to the policy, revocable by another — and the agent paid a bill the treasurer named
from the Safe, inside the limits, with the receipt read back and the decision anchored. The
Zodiac-style module (policy calls the Safe directly, Safe owners as principal) remains the
fuller form for later; this form needs nothing deployed into the Safe.
