Same address on every chain for the owner's contract (PLAN 5.22, docs/59 §3.10) — Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: the hosted service now deploys a tenant's PasskeyPrincipal through the
EIP-7997 keyless CREATE2 factory (0x4e59b44847b379578588920cA78FbF26c0B4956C,
present on Arc testnet) with salt = keccak256("tameion.principal.v1" || qx || qy).
The owner's contract address is a function of the passkey alone: the same on
every chain that has the factory, and deploying twice is a no-op (code already
there → address returned, nothing sent). `arc::ArcClient::deploy_deterministic`,
`arc::predict_create2`, `level1::principal_salt`; the tenant records `owner_tx`.
The policy still deploys with CREATE: its constructor names chain-specific
contracts (the ERC-8004 registry, on Base the USDC token), so its address
cannot be chain-independent without an initializer pattern — recorded, not
done.
