## passes, and a defect they exposed

The first paying pass ran 40 s after its cycle was due and paid it (cycle 748,
at 788). The next pass, 8 s later, paid cycle 808 -- about 15 s early. Under the
then cap (half the interval) that was allowed; on a monthly bill the same
proportion is fifteen days early. The cap is now a tenth of the interval (the
proportion three days is of a month): `within_pay_lead`, 5 tests, Rust 200/200,
acceptance 38/38. Re-run on the same fork with the fixed build, owner's
schedule 748, 808, 868, 928, 988, …:

pass 1: chain now 1790878934 · 2 settled · paid at 1790878947 (cycle 868, overdue)
