A pass when money moves, not only on a clock (PLAN 5.6) — Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: with TAMEION_LEVEL1_WATCH_SECS set, the service checks every tick which tenant wallets or
policies sent or received USDC in the new blocks since the last tick — two eth_getLogs per chain
(sender side, receiver side) over Arc's all-USDC emitter 0xffff…fffe, the new range only, never a
backfill — and starts a pass for each account touched, through the same rate-limited job path a
button press takes (a pass in the last ten minutes answers instead of starting another). At most 3
watcher passes per chain per tick, so a wallet many accounts watch cannot start a pass storm on a
public node (on this machine a dozen demo accounts watch the same treasury); the rest wait for the
clock scheduler.

Code: arc::ArcClient::touched_among(wallets, from, to); the watcher loop in tameion-app (env
TAMEION_LEVEL1_WATCH_SECS; first tick starts at the head; range capped at 2,000 blocks).
