Post-pay read-back (PLAN 5.21, docs/59 §3.15) — anvil acceptance, 2026-10-14

What: after every `pay`, the operator fetches the receipt and requires exactly
one `Paid(to, usdcUnits, actionHash)` from the policy naming what it sent
(`arc::ArcClient::confirm_paid`, pure matcher `arc::paid_log_matches`). The
Settlement record carries the verdict. A mismatch writes HALT beside the
audit log, records an Escalation, stops paying for the rest of the pass, and
every later pass runs in propose-only until a human removes the file. The
hedge layer's "no order is done until the position is read back" rule,
applied to payments.

Unit test (arc): a Paid log is matched field by field — a different amount,
payee or action hash is a mismatch; another contract's log, or a USDC
Transfer from the policy, is not a Paid and is ignored.

Live on anvil, scripts/acceptance.sh (53/53 checks after the change):
  event log log-anvil/0xdded3cbdc19095a2e921b6feebf157819b809f36dfdd8967982d1f9ff44ff402.events.jsonl:

Not yet seen live: a mismatch (the contract has never emitted a Paid that
differs from the call that caused it; the path is exercised by the unit
test). The Arc tenants pick the check up on their next paying pass.
