Arc testnet: chainId 5042002; RPC https://rpc.testnet.arc.network; a second,
independent JSON-RPC at https://explorer.testnet.arc.io/api/eth-rpc (Blockscout;
eth_chainId → 0x4cef52 = 5042002, eth_blockNumber answers).

1. EIP-7910 `eth_config`: NOT supported.
   cast rpc eth_config → "error code -32601: method not supported: eth_config"

2. EIP-7951 / RIP-7212 secp256r1 precompile at 0x…0100: LIVE on Arc testnet.
   Method: a fresh P-256 key (python `cryptography`), sha256 of a probe message,
   ECDSA signature normalised to low-s, input = hash || r || s || x || y (160 bytes),
   `cast call 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000100 <input>`:
     Arc testnet        → 0x…0001  (valid)
     Base Sepolia       → 0x…0001
     Arbitrum Sepolia   → 0x…0001
   (`cast code 0x100` is 0x on every chain: precompiles have no code; the call
   is the probe.) docs/59 §3.9 assumed Arc lacked it; it does not. OpenZeppelin's
   P256 library tries the precompile first and falls back to Solidity, so our
   PasskeyPrincipal verifies on the precompile on Arc. No ask to Circle needed.
