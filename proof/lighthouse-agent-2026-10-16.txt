READ THIS BEFORE TRUSTING THE /agent ROWS
- Every /agent run carries Lighthouse's warning "The page loaded too slowly to finish within the time limit. Results may be incomplete." The page holds two
  Server-Sent Events streams open for as long as it is open, so the network is never idle and Lighthouse stops waiting at 25 s. The audit still ran on the
  live page: the full-page screenshot Lighthouse itself took (extracted from the report JSON and looked at for halt-mobile-light, REAL-gold2329-desktop-light,
  desk REAL-gold2329-mobile-light) shows the finished UI in the right theme.
- Cross-check that does not depend on Lighthouse's wait: axe-core 4.10.2 on the live DOM of 27 page, state, width and theme combinations, no violations
  (proof/design-agent-view-2026-10-16.txt §2).

WHAT WENT WRONG ON THE WAY (docs/60 P-154)
- The first Lighthouse set said 100 for every page. The Chrome it attached to follows the Mac's appearance (dark), so it never audited light; axe on light then found
  6 contrast violations (the inactive Events tabs, the halt banner's secondary lines) and one label-in-name mismatch (weight 0, so the score stayed 100). Fixed.
- The second set was meant to cover both themes by seeding the theme, and the "light" half was still dark: the seed page was opened through the DevTools
  endpoint with an unencoded `&theme=light`, which the endpoint drops. I only saw it by looking at the screenshots Lighthouse had taken. The seed URL is now
  percent-encoded and the nine light runs above were taken again (their screenshots are light). The table above is the corrected one: nine dark, nine light.
