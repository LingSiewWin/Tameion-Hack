== 6. Witness: the live run crashed once (pass B, attempt 1)

What is wrong. scripts/audit_pack.py:261-262 raises on any JSON-RPC error reply, and nothing above it catches the exception
(main() is called bare at line 971). The retry loop at lines 250-260 covers only URLError and TimeoutError. So one refused eth_call
ends the run with a Python traceback, exit 1, and an empty pack directory (os.makedirs at line 536 had run; nothing else was written).
The head comment (lines 28-29) says exit 1 means "one failed (the pack is still written and says which)" and exit 2 means "a tool or
an input is missing". A crash is neither, and exit 1 cannot be told from a failed check without reading the output.

Why (measured, log 40). publicnode's Arbitrum Sepolia node answers eth_call only for blocks up to about 120 blocks old. At 128 blocks
and older it answers -32000 "historical state <hash> is not available". The other node, sepolia-rollup.arbitrum.io, answered at
every age I tried, up to 4000 blocks. The block time here is about a quarter of a second (attempt 2, 3 and 4 read blocks 320189824,
320189972 and 320190104 at 04:55:47, 04:56:24 and 04:56:59: 3.8 to 4.0 blocks a second), so publicnode stops answering about 30
seconds after the block. read_chain picks `at = min(heads)` at line 326, then scans eth_getLogs on both nodes (lines 339-358), and
only then makes the three state reads at `at`: balanceOf (line 360), agent() (line 362), rootOf (line 366). In attempt 1 the
traceback is in line 366, on publicnode, the 6th of those calls: the first five were answered, the sixth fell outside the window.
Attempt 1 lasted 49 seconds in all; attempts 2, 3 and 4 lasted 37, 35 and 37 seconds and passed. I did not time the phases inside
the script, so "the log scan was slower that time" is an inference from these numbers, not a measurement.

What to do instead (not applied; I changed no code). In read_chain, move the three state reads (lines 360-366) up to just after
`at = at or min(heads)` (line 326), before the log scan. They are the only reads that need state at `at`; the log scan needs only
blocks up to `at`. They then happen within a second of choosing `at`, far inside the window. Second, make rpc() (or main()) turn this
RuntimeError into a printed message and exit 2, so a crash cannot be taken for "a check failed". The offline fixture cannot show
either: file:// answers have no clock, which is why the unit tests and test_all.sh line 38 passed every time.
This belongs in docs/60-problems-and-fixes.md; I was not to touch the founder's tree, so the coordinator should log it.
