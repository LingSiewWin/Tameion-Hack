Guardians (PLAN 5.30, docs/59 §3.13: the ERC-7093 idea) — Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: "if you lose your phone". The owner names up to sixteen guardian
addresses on their PasskeyPrincipal — a co-founder, a hardware wallet in a
drawer, a lawyer — and a threshold. Each guardian calls approveRecovery(qx, qy)
for the same replacement key; the threshold-th agreement makes it pending for
the recovery delay, exactly as the single recovery address can; the passkey
cancels it until then; anyone applies it after. A cancel, an apply, or a
change of guardians bumps `recoveryEpoch`, so earlier partial approvals are
void. Guardians can only propose a key; they can never spend. The single
`recovery` address keeps working beside them.

Contract: contracts/src/PasskeyPrincipal.sol — guardians[], guardianThreshold,
recoveryEpoch, guardianApproved / guardianApprovals (by ballot =
keccak(epoch, qx, qy)), setGuardians (only the passkey, through execute),
approveRecovery (guardians), isGuardian, guardianCount, recoveryBallot.
Tests: contracts/test/PasskeyGuardians.t.sol, 7 — only the passkey sets them
and the list is checked (threshold bounds, no duplicates); two of three make
a replacement pending and the passkey cancels it, after which the same two
must agree again; guardians disagreeing on the key never reach the threshold;
after the delay anyone applies and the key rotates; changing the guardians
voids partial approvals; the recovery address still works; an empty list
removes them. forge: 131. `forge lint src`: no findings.

Service / page: inbox `guardians {list, threshold, pendingEta}`;
`POST /api/level1/prepare-guardians {owner, guardians[], threshold}` → calldata
for the passkey; card "If you lose your phone" on `/` (addresses, how many must
agree, "Set with my key"); the decoder describes `setGuardians` in words
("Name 2 guardians; 2 of them may together propose a replacement for your
passkey … they cannot spend"). `scripts/guardian_approve.sh <owner> <qx> <qy>`
for a guardian; `… status` to read.

Chain of custody: the owner names guardians with one tap → two guardians, each a plain key with
no spending power, agree on a replacement → the contract makes it pending for the delay and the
page shows it → the owner cancels with one tap. Had the owner lost the phone, waiting out the
delay and calling applyRecovery would have rotated the key to the guardians' choice.
