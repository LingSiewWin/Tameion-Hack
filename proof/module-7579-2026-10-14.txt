The Tameion limits as an ERC-7579 executor module (PLAN 5.29) — Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: for a company already on a modular smart account (Safe7579, Kernel, Nexus, OpenZeppelin's
AccountERC7579), Tameion is a module it installs, not an account it moves to. `TameionExecutor`
keeps per-account terms — agent key, token, per-payment cap, trailing-24 h cap, reserve floor,
delay — and moves money only through the account's `executeFromExecutor`, which the account
allows only for installed executors. Payees are payable after the delay; Autopilot only after the
delay; narrowing is instant, widening waits; the agent's term lapses; a replay guard per account.
To prove it with nothing but a wallet and a chain, the wallet runs OpenZeppelin's ERC-7579 account
(`TameionModularAccount` = AccountERC7579 + SignerEIP7702) by EIP-7702 and installs the module with
a self-call — no bundler, no EntryPoint.
