The organiser's three sentences as three tests (S21, docs/117 §9). Written 2026-10-17 23:51 +0800.

The organiser's brief (docs/04:89, "Agents and Ledgers"): "A ledger checks that debits equal credits. It does not check that the
vendor was the right one, that the invoice was real, or that a retry didn't pay it twice."

Test file: crates/operator/tests/organiser.rs (branch seat/S21). Each test starts anvil with Arc's chain id 5042002, deploys the policy
from contracts/bytecode/BudgetPolicy.hex (the bytes tameion-app deploys for every account), funds it with 10 USDC, approves one payee and
lets its 60 s cooling-off pass on the chain's clock, writes the account record and agent key the way the service keeps them, and runs the
real tameion-app binary against it. Each test reads the chain (Paid events, balances, executed(actionHash), rootOf) and the account's
files before it reads the service's reply.

- wrong_vendor: the agent pays the approved payee 0.10 USDC (control), then asks to pay an address nobody approved. Checked: no Paid to
  it, its balance 0 wei, executed(op) false, one Paid in total; reply 409 payee_not_approved, executed "no".
- phantom_invoice: the approved payee's own signed invoice and a "phantom" (same payee named, new reference, signed by another key) are
  posted to /api/level1/invoice; then one pass runs and anchors. Checked: the phantom is not in tenants/<policy>.invoices.jsonl and not
  in tenants/<policy>/log/<root>.events.jsonl; the real one is in both; MemoryAnchor.rootOf(agent) equals the record's root; reply
  422 with the reason, executed "no".
- double_pay_on_retry: one payment (0.25 USDC) under one Idempotency-Key, sent four times: the first send's client hangs up before the
  reply (a timeout); the retry and one more send come back 200 with Idempotent-Replayed: true; a second copy of the service on its own
  disk (no replay store, no payments file) answers from the chain's executed(actionHash). Checked: one Paid for the action hash,
  executed true, the payee holds exactly 0.25 USDC; every reply names the same transaction.

== What this does not show ==
- anvil, not a public testnet: the transaction hashes above exist only on the test's throwaway chain. The same route and the same
  contract checks (BudgetPolicyBase._payChecked, shared by the native and the token policy) ran live on Arbitrum Sepolia in
  proof/mcp-http-pay-2026-10-17.txt.
- phantom_invoice covers an invoice signed by a key that is not the payee's. An invoice signed by an unknown payee's own key is a
  real invoice from a stranger: the service records it and the payee still needs the owner's passkey (docs/65 §5.4); that is a trust
  decision, not a forgery.
- The in-flight retry (409 idempotency_pending) is handled by the test's retry loop; in these runs the first send had finished within
  300 ms, so the retry was answered from the replay store at once (0 x 409).
