Agent authority that lapses (PLAN 5.24, docs/59 §3.4: the EIP-8130 shape) — Arc testnet, 2026-10-14

What: the agent's key may pay for 90 days from deploy (`agentExpiry`); after
that `pay` is refused with `AgentLapsed(expiry)` until the owner renews with
`renewAgent(until)` — principal only, instant, at most a year ahead, and an
earlier date than today's expiry is the owner's brake. A key nobody
re-confirms in a quarter is one nobody is watching. Standing orders and the
owner's own settlements never depend on it: they are the owner's instructions,
not the agent's. Operator: a lapsed agent proposes and does not try to pay.
Inbox: `agentAuthority {lapsesAt, lapsed, daysLeft, renewSuggested (inside 14
days), renewUntil}`. Page: a card in the last fourteen days, loud when lapsed,
one tap "Renew for 90 days" (passkey). `POST /api/level1/prepare-renew`.
The page's decoder describes `renewAgent` in words ("Let the agent keep paying
until <date> … an earlier date is your brake").

Tests: contracts/test/BudgetPolicyAgentExpiry.t.sol, 6 — term starts at 90
days and the agent pays inside it; after the term the agent is refused by
name; the principal renews instantly and nobody else can; renewal is bounded
to a year and must be in the future; shortening is the brake and renewing
restores payment; a lapsed agent stops neither a standing order nor the owner.
forge: 118 tests.
