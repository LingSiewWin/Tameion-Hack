# The agent asks the passkey, and stops paying itself forward — local chain, 2026-10-02

Anvil :8546, chain 31337. A `BudgetPolicyToken` whose principal is a
`PasskeyPrincipal` (Chrome passkey, virtual authenticator, UV on); a six-decimal
test token; the agent is anvil key 1, the relayer anvil key 0, and the owner's
old wallet anvil key 2. Driven by `scripts/prove_passkey.sh local …`; the agent
is `operator/examples/run_arc.rs` (rule-based reasoner, no model). Final build:
Rust 198/198, acceptance 38/38.

What it shows, in order:
1. The agent learns two monthly bills from the owner's OLD WALLET
   (`Config::also_history`), pays the one the passkey attested, and for the
   one it never attested hands over a passkey approval link. That link's
   challenge, computed in Rust (`arc::passkey_challenge`), equals the one the
   contract computes (`challengeFor`).
2. One approval in the browser; the next pass pays the new payee.
3. An immediate re-run pays nothing; a month later, exactly one cycle of each,
   "due in 1.0 days", on the owner's schedule; the re-run after that, nothing.

Point 3 is the fix of a defect this loop found. On the build before it, pass 2
paid 0x1111 a second time ("due in 30.0 days"): the agent's own payment had
become the newest data point, the next cycle was projected from the day the
agent paid, it landed inside the 30-day horizon, and it was paid at once -- and
would have been again on every pass, bounded only by the daily cap. That pass,
on the old build:

The fix (operator/src/lib.rs): the policy's `Paid` events are read
(`ArcClient::agent_payments`) and those transactions are not evidence; each
obligation steps past cycles already `executed` on chain
(`step_past_settled_cycles`); and a bill is paid only once it falls due within
`pay_lead` (3 days), capped at half its interval (`within_pay_lead`, 4 tests).
Everything below is that build, from a fresh deployment.

Later on 10-02 the cap became a tenth of the interval (the fork rehearsal
showed half was too generous; see arbitrum-fork-rehearsal-2026-10-02.txt). On
this loop's monthly cadence both caps give the same 3-day lead -- min(3 d,
15 d) then, min(3 d, 3 d) now -- so every decision below is unchanged.
