# Clear-signing through the Secure Enclave bridge: the bridge computes what the chip signs from the decision's fields and

# written 2026-10-16 02:34:20 +0800 · desk desk-init-0132 (0x048a9a549f19be4aa04a84dbcc51007f57d0160a, Arbitrum Sepolia, no venue)

## What changed
- apps/desk-se/main.swift: `sign <challenge> --reason "<text>"` opens the key with an LAContext whose localizedReason is the text
  (SecureEnclave.P256.Signing.PrivateKey(dataRepresentation:authenticationContext:)); compiled with swiftc, selftest 16/16.
- scripts/desk_se_bridge.py: POST /sign {"decision": {kind, chainId, desk, issued, …}} → the challenge from target/debug/examples/
  desk_challenge (the Rust the service verifies with) and the sentence from the same fields; returns the challenge it signed.
  A raw challenge (the account's other owner actions) gets "Sign a Tameion request from this Mac (no details given)";
  --clear-only refuses those.
- apps/console/src/lib/tameion.ts passkeyAssert(…, decision): sends the decision, and refuses the assertion if the bridge's
  challenge is not the page's own (guard.ts). Callers: approve a counterparty, the unhedged base, an unsigned step, the offset.
